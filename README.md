## 📊 Overview & Market Dynamics

The global **Media Monitoring & Intelligence Market** is estimated at **$6.0 billion – $6.5 billion (2026)** and projected to reach over **$15 billion by 2032** (growing at a CAGR of ~12–15%). 

**🏗️ Market Structure:** The sector is **moderately fragmented**. While enterprise legacy giants (LexisNexis, Dow Jones, Cision, Meltwater) capture a substantial share of corporate PR and licensed print/broadcast budgets, dozens of specialized SaaS tools, AI-native social listening engines, and regional media monitoring platforms serve niche segments. Consolidation via private equity is ongoing, yet the rapid influx of LLM-driven news scraping and sentiment analysis tools keeps entry barriers low for dynamic sub-segments.

## ⚙️ Key Features to Consider

When building or evaluating media monitoring software:

- 📡 **Coverage Breadth:** Online news, licensed print media, broadcast (TV/radio audio transcription), social media (X/Twitter, Reddit, YouTube, TikTok), podcasts, and blogs.
- 🔔 **Real-Time Alerting:** Immediate webhooks, Slack/Teams alerts, or instant email digests when sentiment drops or brand mention spikes occur.
- 🧠 **Sentiment & Entity Extraction:** AI/NLP models to categorize mentions (positive/negative/neutral), identify key influencers, and extract brand entities.
- 📈 **Analytics & Share of Voice (SoV):** Dashboard reports measuring brand visibility relative to competitors over time.
