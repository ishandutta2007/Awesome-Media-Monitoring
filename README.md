# Awesome-Media-Monitoring

## Top Media Monitoring Platforms Ecosystem

**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**

*Focused on News Monitoring, Broadcast Tracking, Social Listening, Brand Mentions, Sentiment & Media Intelligence*

**Last updated: September 2026**



This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** for **Media Monitoring**. These systems track brand, competitor, and topic mentions across news, broadcast, social, and online sources, delivering alerts, sentiment analysis, and media intelligence reports.



**Examples** include Meltwater, Cision, Determ, TVEyes, Signal AI, Critical Mention, Onclusive, LexisNexis Newsdesk, Factiva, and Truescope (the category leaders).



**Open-source emphasis**: Full commercial-grade media monitoring (especially broadcast/TV and licensed news archives) has limited pure open-source equivalents. Practical open options focus on **RSS/news aggregation**, **social listening prototypes**, **OSINT dashboards**, and self-hosted mention trackers. This section lists the strongest available open resources and is realistic about coverage gaps.



Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## SaaS/Hosted Platforms

- **[Meltwater](https://www.meltwater.com/)**  

  Enterprise media intelligence platform monitoring news, social, broadcast, and online sources with AI-assisted insights, sentiment, and reporting.



- **[Cision](https://www.cision.com/)**  

  Media monitoring and PR platform combining coverage tracking with journalist databases and press-release distribution (PR Newswire).



- **[Determ](https://www.determ.com/)**  

  Media and social listening platform focused on real-time brand and topic monitoring with analytics and alerts.



- **[TVEyes](https://www.tveyes.com/)**  

  Broadcast media monitoring specialist tracking television and radio mentions across markets.



- **[Signal AI](https://www.signal-ai.com/)**  

  AI-powered media and reputation intelligence platform for monitoring news and extracting actionable insights.



- **[Critical Mention](https://www.criticalmention.com/)**  

  Real-time broadcast and online media monitoring platform popular for TV, radio, and digital coverage tracking.



- **[Onclusive](https://www.onclusive.com/)**  

  Media monitoring and analytics platform providing coverage tracking, measurement, and insights for communications teams.



- **[LexisNexis Newsdesk](https://www.lexisnexis.com/)**  

  Professional news monitoring and research service drawing on extensive licensed news archives and sources.



- **[Factiva (Dow Jones)](https://www.dowjones.com/factiva/)**  

  Premium news and business information platform widely used for media monitoring and research from trusted sources.



- **[Truescope](https://www.truescope.com/)**  

  Media monitoring and insights platform serving brands and agencies with coverage tracking and analytics.



## Open-Source GitHub Projects

- **[MediaWatch and similar news network analysis tools](https://github.com/cvcio/mediawatch)**  

  Open-source platforms for monitoring online media outlets, clustering articles, and analyzing information flows (useful for disinformation and media research).



- **[RSS and news aggregation open frameworks](https://github.com/)**  

  Self-hosted RSS readers, feed aggregators, and keyword-alert systems that form the foundation of many custom news monitors.



- **[OSINT and global news dashboard projects](https://github.com/)**  

  Open dashboards that aggregate real-time news, geopolitical signals, and public sources for monitoring and research.



- **[Social listening and mention open trackers](https://github.com/)**  

  Community tools for tracking brand or keyword mentions across public social platforms and web sources.



- **[Google News / public API monitoring scripts](https://github.com/)**  

  Open scripts and small applications that monitor Google News and similar public endpoints for keyword and brand alerts.



- **[Sentiment analysis open libraries](https://github.com/)**  

  NLP libraries (spaCy, Hugging Face models, VADER, etc.) used to score sentiment on collected media and social text.



- **[Web scraping and content extraction open tools](https://github.com/)**  

  Frameworks for legally collecting and parsing public web and news content into structured monitoring pipelines.



- **[Alerting and notification open systems](https://github.com/)**  

  Tools that push keyword matches via email, Slack, or webhooks when new relevant coverage appears.



- **[Media archive and search open prototypes](https://github.com/)**  

  Experimental self-hosted indexes of public news and social content for historical monitoring queries.



- **[Brand monitoring open starter kits](https://github.com/)**  

  Starter repositories that combine feed collection, keyword filtering, basic sentiment, and simple dashboards.



### Additional Strong Open-Source Options

- Building a lightweight monitor with **RSS aggregators + keyword filters + sentiment libraries + alerting**.

- Using **OSINT dashboards** and public news APIs for research-grade or internal monitoring.

- Combining open collection pipelines with commercial licensed sources when broadcast or premium news is required.

- Accepting that comprehensive broadcast (TV/radio) coverage, licensed news archives, large-scale social firehoses, and polished PR reporting still require commercial platforms (Meltwater, Cision, TVEyes, Critical Mention, Factiva, LexisNexis, etc.).

- Focusing open-source efforts on cost control, data ownership, and monitoring of public web and social sources.



**Frameworks for building custom systems**: Collect public feeds and social data → filter by keywords/brands → apply open sentiment and entity models → store and alert → visualize in a simple dashboard. Suitable for startups, research teams, and organizations with modest monitoring needs. Most PR and corporate communications teams continue to rely on commercial media monitoring platforms for breadth and reliability of sources.
